# The Asclepion

A free MCAT resource hub — a curated list of prep material sorted by what's free, what's worth paying for, and where premeds actually talk to each other, plus a built-in study schedule generator.

Named after the ancient Greek healing temples dedicated to Asclepius, which doubled as the medical schools of their time.

## What's inside

- **Free** — official AAMC resources, Khan Academy's MCAT course, Jack Westin's daily CARS passages, community Anki decks, and free full-length practice exams.
- **Paid & Official** — UWorld's QBank, Kaplan's courses, and AAMC's official paid prep products.
- **Community** — links to r/Mcat and r/premed.
- **Study Schedule** — enter a test date, study days per week, and hours per day, and it generates a phased calendar (content review → practice → full-length exams) that can be downloaded as an `.ics` file or printed.
- **AI Flashcard Generator** — in progress, not live yet.

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step, no dependencies besides Google Fonts (Fraunces, Inter, IBM Plex Mono). The whole thing is a single file.

## Running it locally

There's nothing to install. Download `index.html` and open it in a browser, or clone the repo and do the same.

## Notes

Resource links are checked periodically but pricing and availability can change — treat this as a starting point, not a guarantee. The study schedule uses a general phase-split heuristic (roughly 50% content review, 30% practice, 20% full-lengths and final review), not a personalized study plan.
